---
name: grill-me
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a plan or design until reaching shared understanding, resolving each branch of the decision tree. Use when user wants to stress-test a plan, get grilled on their design, or mentions "grill me".
---

用繁體中文（廣東話書面語）進行訪談。以中文提問、中文輸出建議答案。技術術語、代碼、變數名保留英文。

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until
we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design
tree resolving dependencies between decisions one by one.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore
the codebase instead.

For each question, provide your recommended answer.

**Ask ONE question at a time.** Never batch multiple questions in a single
turn. Pose exactly one question (with its recommended answer), wait for my
reply, then ask the next one based on what I said. This keeps the decision
tree focused and lets each answer shape the following question.
